Starting with [9.2.4-1](/blog/2025/10/09/qemu-arm-v9-2-4-1-released/), 
the `qemu-system-gnuarmeclipse` binary is no 
longer provided, as it is now deprecated. 

The final version that includes it is 
[8.2.6-1](/blog/2024/09/12/qemu-arm-v8-2-6-1-released/), 
released on 2024-09-12.
